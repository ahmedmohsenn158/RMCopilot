# Revolving Credit

## What it is
A reusable credit line for short-term liquidity. Customers can draw, repay, and redraw within the approved limit.

## RM guidance
If utilisation exceeds 90%, discuss repayment timing, upcoming cash inflows, and whether the limit still matches the customer's working-capital cycle.

## Breach trigger
Utilisation above 100% requires immediate RM review and Credit notification.
