# Trade Finance Facility

## What it is
A revolving facility that supports import letters of credit, guarantees, and short-term working-capital needs for SME and corporate clients.

## Eligibility
- Active business account
- Updated commercial registration and tax documents
- Satisfactory credit review
- At least 12 months of operating history

## Renewal
Start renewal no later than 30 days before expiry. The RM submits a renewal request, Credit reviews the file, the customer signs the revised facility letter, and Operations reinstates the limit.
