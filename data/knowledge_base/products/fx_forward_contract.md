# FX Forward Contract

## What it is
A forward contract locks in today's exchange rate for a future transaction. It is useful for customers with recurring import or export exposure.

## Eligibility
- Active current account for 6+ months
- Minimum transaction size: USD 10,000
- SME Gold, Corporate, or higher-value Retail customers with documented FX exposure

## Pricing
- Premium: 0.3% to 0.8% of notional depending on tenor and risk
- Tenors: 30, 60, 90, and 180 days

## Pitch trigger signals
- 3+ USD inflows or outflows in a 30-day window
- Customer mentions import suppliers or export contracts
- Significant EGP/USD rate movement in the past 7 days

## RM talking point
"Given your recent USD activity, locking in today's rate can protect your margin from a 2% to 3% rate swing over the next quarter."
